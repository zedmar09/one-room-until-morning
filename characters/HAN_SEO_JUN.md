# Han Seo-jun — Character File

## Identity

**Name:** Han Seo-jun  
**Age:** 18  
**Role:** Quiet honor student  
**Height:** 174 cm  
**Build:** Slim, graceful, lightly delicate while still clearly masculine

## Face

- Fair, clear skin
- Refined young masculine facial structure
- Narrow, clean jawline
- Straight nose
- Subtle lips
- Dark gray-brown eyes
- Calm, observant gaze
- Attractive but understated appearance

## Hair

- Soft black hair
- Straight, natural texture
- Medium-short manhwa hairstyle
- Slightly loose fringe near the eyes
- Individual strand definition is allowed, but do not make it glossy or reflective
- Hair shape and length must remain fixed throughout the story

## Personality

- Reserved
- Composed
- Observant
- Emotionally careful
- Intelligent
- Quietly sensitive
- Easily flustered by direct affection
- Never loud or theatrically shy

## School Uniform

- Crisp white long-sleeve school shirt
- Dark navy tie, properly fixed
- Charcoal / very dark gray trousers
- Plain black school shoes
- Simple dark school bag

Uniform colors and design must remain unchanged across all story images.

## Body Language

- Upright but relaxed posture
- Controlled movements
- Often holds his school-bag strap when uncertain
- Looks away when embarrassed
- Small hand tension when emotionally affected
- Does not use exaggerated gestures

## Expression Language

Preferred expressions:

- slight widening of the eyes
- faint blush
- lowered gaze
- lips slightly parted in surprise
- softened gaze
- very small smile only when emotionally appropriate

Avoid:

- chibi embarrassment
- giant sweat drops
- comedic shock
- exaggerated open-mouth reactions
- heavy blush effects

## Fixed Continuity Rules

Never change:

- black hair
- hairstyle
- dark gray-brown eyes
- fair skin
- facial proportions
- slim body type
- height
- uniform colors
- neat visual presentation

Seo-jun must always be visibly shorter than Yoo Min-jae.

## Character Reference Generation

When generating Seo-jun's approved reference image:

- generate Seo-jun alone
- neutral or simple background
- no dialogue
- no story action
- no dramatic effects
- normal school uniform
- clearly show his face
- include a clean portrait or three-quarter view
- optionally include a full-body view if composition permits
- use the project's flat, premium manhwa rendering rules
- prioritize a reusable, easy-to-recognize design over a dramatic illustration
