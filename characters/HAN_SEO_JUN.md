# Han Seo-jun — Character File

## Identity

**Name:** Han Seo-jun  
**Age:** 22  
**Role:** University student; reserved, precise, emotionally guarded  
**Height:** 175 cm  
**Build:** Slim, graceful, lightly toned, clearly adult and masculine

## Face

- Fair, clear skin
- Refined adult masculine facial structure
- Narrow clean jawline
- Straight nose
- Subtle lips
- Dark gray-brown eyes
- Calm, observant gaze
- Attractive but understated appearance
- Slightly sharper maturity than a high-school character

## Hair

- Soft black hair
- Straight natural texture
- Medium-short layered manhwa hairstyle
- Slightly loose fringe near the eyes
- Selected strand definition only
- No broad glossy highlight bands
- No wet-look shine even during rain scenes
- Hair shape and length must remain consistent

## Personality

- Reserved
- Composed
- Intelligent
- Emotionally careful
- Dry sense of humor
- Quietly sensitive
- Easily flustered when affection becomes direct
- Avoids vulnerable conversations until forced to confront them
- Never childish or theatrically shy

## Canonical Story Outfit

Use this outfit for the entire storm-night sequence unless the storyboard explicitly says otherwise:

- charcoal lightweight coat, worn open
- white or warm-white button-up shirt
- dark straight-cut trousers
- simple black leather shoes
- dark crossbody or shoulder bag
- minimal silver wristwatch

After entering the hotel, the coat may be removed because it is damp. The white shirt and dark trousers remain the canonical indoor outfit.

Clothing must remain opaque, matte, tasteful, and consistent. Rain may make fabric look damp, but never transparent or overly clingy.

## Body Language

- Upright but relaxed posture
- Controlled movements
- Often looks away when embarrassed
- Fingers tighten subtly when emotionally affected
- Holds his own arms or bag strap when defensive
- When he finally acts, the motion should feel decisive because he is normally restrained

## Expression Language

Preferred:

- slight eye widening
- faint blush
- lowered gaze
- lips slightly parted in surprise
- sharpened stare during arguments
- softened gaze after emotional release
- very small smile when genuinely relieved

Avoid:

- chibi embarrassment
- giant sweat drops
- comedic shock
- exaggerated open-mouth expressions
- heavy blush effects

## Relationship Behavior

Seo-jun wants Min-jae but has spent six months convincing himself that the almost-kiss was a mistake.

His attraction should appear through:

- looking away after accidentally staring
- tense jaw or fingers
- guarded replies
- involuntary proximity
- being the one who grabs Min-jae's shirt in the turning-point scene
- ultimately choosing to close the final distance during the kiss

## Fixed Continuity Rules

Never change:

- black hair
- hairstyle
- dark gray-brown eyes
- fair skin
- facial proportions
- slim adult build
- height
- canonical clothing palette
- restrained visual demeanor

Seo-jun must always be visibly shorter than Yoo Min-jae.
