# Character Bible — After the Bell

This document is the permanent source of truth for character appearance and personality. Do not reinterpret or redesign these characters from image to image.

---

## Han Seo-jun

**Role:** Quiet honor student  
**Age:** 18  
**Height:** 174 cm  
**Build:** Slim, graceful, lightly delicate without appearing feminine  
**Skin:** Fair, clear complexion  
**Hair:** Soft black hair; straight and natural; slightly loose fringe near the eyes  
**Eyes:** Dark gray-brown  
**Face:** Refined young masculine features, narrow clean jawline, straight nose, subtle lips, calm expressive eyes

### Personality

- Reserved
- Composed
- Observant
- Emotionally careful
- Easily flustered when directly confronted with affection
- Speaks only when necessary
- Does not exaggerate reactions

### School Uniform

- Crisp white long-sleeve school shirt
- Dark navy tie, properly fixed
- Charcoal or very dark gray trousers
- Plain black school shoes
- Simple dark school bag

The uniform must remain consistent throughout the story.

### Body Language

- Upright but not stiff
- Often holds his bag strap when uncertain
- Looks away when embarrassed
- Small changes in gaze carry most of his emotion
- Hands and posture remain controlled

### Expression Language

Use subtle expressions only:

- Slight widening of the eyes
- Faint blush
- Small downward glance
- Lips parting slightly in surprise
- Softened gaze toward the ending

### Never Change

- Black hair
- Hair length or fringe structure
- Dark gray-brown eyes
- Fair skin
- Slim proportions
- Refined facial structure
- Neat styling
- Height relationship: he is always visibly shorter than Min-jae

## Yoo Min-jae

**Role:** Warm, quietly affectionate classmate  
**Age:** 18  
**Height:** 183 cm  
**Build:** Lean, lightly athletic  
**Skin:** Warm fair / light beige  
**Hair:** Dark brown, soft, slightly tousled  
**Eyes:** Warm brown  
**Face:** Handsome and approachable, clean masculine jaw, relaxed eyes, gentle smile

### Personality

- Calm
- Sincere
- Patient
- Quietly confident
- Attentive
- Affectionate without being loud
- Never arrogant or pushy
- Notices Seo-jun carefully

### School Uniform

- Same school uniform system as Seo-jun
- White long-sleeve school shirt
- Dark navy tie
- Charcoal or very dark gray trousers
- Plain black school shoes
- Sleeves may be slightly rolled for a relaxed contrast
- Collar can be a little less formal, but still neat

Once the exact sleeve/collar treatment is established in Image 1, preserve it through all subsequent images.

### Body Language

- Relaxed shoulders
- Comfortable eye contact
- Slight forward attention toward Seo-jun
- Calm movements
- Never crowds or corners Seo-jun aggressively

### Expression Language

- Small knowing smile
- Soft direct eye contact
- Gentle seriousness during the confession
- Quiet happiness near the ending

### Never Change

- Dark brown hair
- Hair length and tousled shape
- Warm brown eyes
- Warm fair skin tone
- Lean athletic proportions
- Height relationship: always visibly taller than Seo-jun

## Relationship Visual Rules

The romance is communicated through:

- eye contact
- physical distance gradually closing
- quiet pauses
- subtle blush
- shoulder proximity
- small gestures

Avoid:

- exaggerated heart effects
- dramatic grabbing
- forced intimacy
- chibi reactions
- comedic deformation
- excessive physical contact

### Distance Progression

- Images 1–2: visible physical distance
- Image 3: Min-jae begins closing the distance
- Images 4–6: emotionally intimate framing
- Image 7: walking shoulder-to-shoulder
- Image 8: comfortable, natural closeness

The progression should feel earned rather than sudden.
