# Visual Style Guide — After the Bell

This document is the permanent visual source of truth for the project.

---

## Core Style

Modern Korean romance manhwa / webtoon aesthetic.

Use:

- clean digital linework
- soft cel shading
- polished character rendering
- elegant young male character designs
- realistic-to-stylized human proportions
- expressive eyes
- subtle facial acting
- cinematic framing
- refined, uncluttered backgrounds
- gentle depth of field when appropriate

The images should feel like still frames from a high-quality romance webtoon rather than isolated AI illustrations.

---

## Output Format

**Aspect Ratio:** 9:16 vertical  
**Target:** TikTok / short-form vertical video slideshow

### Safe Composition

Keep important faces, gestures, and speech bubbles away from:

- extreme top edge
- extreme bottom edge
- far left/right margins

Allow visual breathing room for TikTok UI overlays.

---

## Visual Tone

The story should feel:

- quiet
- tender
- intimate
- restrained
- slightly melancholic at first
- warm by the ending
- cinematic rather than flashy

---

## Rendering

Preferred:

- precise but gentle line art
- soft cel-shaded skin
- restrained highlights
- natural fabric folds
- detailed but non-distracting hair
- believable school architecture
- warm environmental lighting

Avoid:

- photorealism
- 3D-rendered appearance
- glossy skin
- plastic-looking characters
- hyper-muscular anatomy
- thick Western comic outlines
- chibi
- neon
- game-poster lighting
- heavy bloom
- excessive lens flare
- oversaturation

---

## Color Palette

### Primary

- warm cream whites
- charcoal gray
- dark navy
- soft brown
- muted gold
- dusty blue-gray

### Lighting Progression

**Images 1–3**
- warm late-afternoon sunlight
- golden window light
- soft neutral classroom shadows

**Images 4–6**
- deeper golden-hour warmth
- more pronounced warm/cool contrast
- slightly dimmer classroom

**Image 7**
- warm hallway reflections
- long soft shadows
- transition toward evening blue

**Image 8**
- soft blue-gold early evening
- calm exterior ambience
- warm skin highlights

---

## Environment

### Classroom

Modern Korean-style high school classroom:

- clean desks
- large windows
- neutral walls
- simple classroom fixtures
- understated academic environment

Do not overcrowd the background.

### Hallway

- clean modern school corridor
- long perspective
- windows or warm reflected light
- mostly empty after school

### School Gate

- believable modern high school entrance
- calm early-evening atmosphere
- no large crowd
- minimal background distraction

---

## Camera Language

Use varied cinematic framing across the story:

- establishing medium-wide shot
- balanced two-shot
- emotional close-up
- profile two-shot
- walking wide shot
- final cinematic medium-wide or wide shot

Avoid repeating the exact same camera angle.

### Romantic Framing

Use:

- eye-line matches
- negative space
- foreground/background depth
- soft focus transitions
- shoulder proximity
- hand details sparingly

Do not use:

- extreme fisheye
- dramatic action angles
- exaggerated Dutch angles
- overly sensual framing

---

## Speech Bubbles

When text is requested:

- use clean, elegant manhwa speech bubbles
- keep typography readable
- preserve exact dialogue
- avoid blocking faces
- use natural reading order
- avoid oversized bubbles

If accurate text rendering would substantially harm the image, generate the artwork cleanly and place the dialogue in a visually appropriate empty area only if the image system can render it reliably. Never invent replacement dialogue.

---

## Emotional Direction

Expressions must remain understated.

Seo-jun communicates emotion primarily through:

- gaze
- blush
- hand tension
- slightly altered posture

Min-jae communicates emotion primarily through:

- steady eye contact
- small smile
- relaxed posture
- gentle seriousness

The romantic atmosphere should come from composition and behavior rather than effects.

---

## Continuity Rules

For every new image:

1. Review the character bible.
2. Review the current story scene.
3. Preserve established uniform details.
4. Preserve hairstyle and facial structure.
5. Preserve height difference.
6. Preserve the current time-of-day progression.
7. Check props carried in the previous image.
8. Do not introduce unexplained wardrobe changes.
9. Use the previous accepted image as an additional visual continuity reference when available.
