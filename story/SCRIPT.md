## Scene 8 — What We Thought

**MIN-JAE:**  
"Do you seriously think I spent six months avoiding you because I didn't want it?"

**SEO-JUN:**

**MIN-JAE:**

## Scene 9 — Tomorrow

**SEO-JUN:**  
"Doesn't matter now."

**MIN-JAE:**  
"Why?"

**SEO-JUN:**

## Scene 10 — Something Honest

**MIN-JAE:**  
"So that's it?"

**SEO-JUN:**  
"What do you want me to say?"

**MIN-JAE:**  
"Something honest."

**SEO-JUN:**  
"You don't get to ask me for honesty the night before you leave."

## Scene 11 — Stay Still

**MIN-JAE:**  
"Your hair's still wet."

**SEO-JUN:**  
"It'll dry."

**MIN-JAE:**  
"Can I?"

**SEO-JUN:**  
"...Yes."

**MIN-JAE:**  
"You know what I should've asked that night?"

**SEO-JUN:**  
"What?"

**MIN-JAE:**  
"If you wanted me closer."

**SEO-JUN:**  
"And if I'd said yes?"

**MIN-JAE:**  
"I would've stayed."

**SEO-JUN:**  
"Then stop stepping away now."
