# Story Script — After the Bell

## Metadata

**Title:** After the Bell  
**Type:** Standalone BL one-shot  
**Genre:** Boys' Love / School Romance / Soft Emotional Tension  
**Total Images:** 8  
**Format:** 9:16 vertical manhwa images  
**Ending:** Complete  

---

## Synopsis

Han Seo-jun, a reserved honor student, often stays late at school for committee work. One evening, he notices that his classmate Yoo Min-jae is still in the classroom long after everyone else has left.

Seo-jun assumes it is coincidence.

It is not.

Min-jae has been staying late because Seo-jun always walks home alone, and Min-jae wants to be the person who walks beside him.

The entire story unfolds in one quiet evening and ends with them leaving school together.

# IMAGE 1 — You're Still Here?

## Purpose

Opening hook and atmosphere.

## Location

Classroom.

## Time

Late afternoon.

## Scene

The classroom is mostly empty after school.

Seo-jun has finished his committee work and is standing beside his desk with his school bag, preparing to leave.

Min-jae is still seated near the window.

Warm sunlight enters through the classroom windows.

## Characters

### Seo-jun

- Standing beside his desk
- Holding his bag
- Mildly curious
- Looking toward Min-jae

### Min-jae

- Sitting near the window
- Relaxed posture
- Calm expression
- Looking back toward Seo-jun

## Camera

Medium-wide establishing composition.

Both characters must be clearly visible while the empty classroom atmosphere remains important.

## Dialogue

**Seo-jun:**  
"You're still here?"

**Min-jae:**  
"I could ask you the same."

## Continuity State After Image

- Seo-jun carries his own bag.
- Min-jae is still seated.
- Both wear their standard school uniforms.
- Sunset remains bright and warm.

# IMAGE 2 — Then Why Are You Here?

## Purpose

Introduce the mystery behind Min-jae staying late.

## Location

Same classroom.

## Time

Several moments after Image 1.

## Scene

Seo-jun remains standing.

He lightly grips his bag strap while speaking.

Min-jae leans back slightly in his chair, still calm.

## Emotion

Seo-jun is puzzled.

Min-jae appears to know more than he is saying.

## Camera

Medium two-shot.

Closer and more intimate than Image 1 while still preserving some distance between them.

## Dialogue

**Seo-jun:**  
"I stay late because of committee work."

**Min-jae:**  
"I know."

**Seo-jun:**  
"...Then why are you here?"

## Continuity State After Image

- Seo-jun still has his bag.
- Min-jae remains seated.
- Physical distance remains between them.
- Sunset is beginning to deepen.

## Location

## Time

## Scene

Seo-jun was not expecting a personal answer.

## Body Language

- Gentle seriousness
- Direct eye contact

- Body still

Medium shot or cinematic two-shot.

Min-jae should now feel more visually present than in Images 1–2.

## Dialogue

**Min-jae:**  
"Because you always leave alone."

## Continuity State After Image

- Min-jae is now standing.
- The emotional dynamic has changed.
- Physical distance may be slightly smaller.

## Continuity State After Image

## Purpose

Main romantic confession.

## Location

## Scene

- Sincere

## Camera

## Dialogue

**Seo-jun:**  
"Why?"

"Because I want to be the one who walks home with you."

## Continuity State After Image

- Seo-jun still carries his bag.
- Min-jae remains gentle and non-aggressive.

# IMAGE 7 — I'm Noticing Now

## Purpose

Romantic payoff and transition from confession to companionship.

## Location

School hallway.

## Time

Early evening.

## Scene

Seo-jun and Min-jae walk side-by-side through a mostly empty school hallway.

Their shoulders are close enough to almost brush.

Long evening shadows stretch across the corridor.

## Body Language

### Seo-jun

- Still shy
- No longer emotionally distant
- Walking comfortably beside Min-jae

### Min-jae

- Relaxed
- Quietly happy
- Matching Seo-jun's walking pace

## Camera

Medium-wide walking shot or cinematic wide side/rear perspective.

Use hallway depth to create a peaceful romantic composition.

## Dialogue

**Seo-jun:**  
"How long have you been waiting for me?"

**Min-jae:**  
"Long enough to know you never noticed."

**Seo-jun:**  
"...I'm noticing now."

## Continuity State After Image

- Both have left the classroom.
- They are walking together.
- Seo-jun still carries his own bag at the beginning of this scene.
- Emotional tension has become mutual warmth.

# IMAGE 8 — Because I Won't

## Purpose

Complete the one-shot with a warm romantic ending.

## Location

Outside the school gate.

## Time

Early evening / blue-gold hour.

## Scene

Min-jae and Seo-jun are leaving school together.

At some point after Image 7, Min-jae has gently taken Seo-jun's school bag and is carrying it for him.

Seo-jun looks toward Min-jae with a shy, softened expression.

The school behind them is quiet.

## Body Language

### Min-jae

- Carrying Seo-jun's bag naturally
- Calm and content
- Walking beside him

### Seo-jun

- Hands freer now
- Slight blush
- Soft gaze
- Comfortable beside Min-jae

## Camera

Cinematic medium-wide or wide final composition.

Both characters should be clearly visible.

The scene should feel complete, not like a cliffhanger.

## Dialogue

**Min-jae:**  
"Then let me walk with you tomorrow too."

**Seo-jun:**  
"...You don't have to wait."

**Min-jae:**  
"Good."

**Min-jae:**  
"Because I won't."

## Ending Caption

"He walked beside him as if he had always belonged there."

## Final Continuity State

The story is complete.

Do not add:
- a sequel tease
- a "Part 2"
- another character
- a cliffhanger
- additional confession dialogue

The emotional ending is simply that they now walk home together.
